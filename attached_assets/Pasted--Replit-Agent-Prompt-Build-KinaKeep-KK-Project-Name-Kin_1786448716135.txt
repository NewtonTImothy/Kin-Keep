# Replit Agent Prompt — Build KinaKeep (KK)

## Project Name

**KinaKeep (KK) — SME Digital Bookkeeping & Receipt Management System**

## Product Tagline

**“Keep Every Kina Accounted For.”**

## Product Vision

Build a simple, modern, mobile-first web application called **KinaKeep (KK)** designed specifically to help Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in Papua New Guinea record, organize, and understand their business finances.

KinaKeep is **not intended to be complicated accounting software**. Its primary purpose is to help SME owners answer three simple questions:

1. **How much money came into my business?**
2. **How much money went out of my business?**
3. **Do I have proof/receipts for my transactions?**

The application should function as a **digital cashbook + receipt filing system + simple business reporting dashboard**.

The first version should focus on simplicity, reliability, usability, and a clean professional interface.
